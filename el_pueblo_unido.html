About this song

"¡El pueblo unido jamás será vencido!" (Translation: "The people united will never be defeated") is a Chilean protest song composed by Sergio Ortega Alvarado and the text written in conjunction with the Quilapayún band, originally recorded in 1973. The song has been adopted and adapted by liberation movements all over the world

Lyrics

Original

El pueblo unido jamás será vencido,

Será mejor

Translation

Verse 1:

The people united will never be defeated,
The people united will never be defeated...

Arise, sing
We are going to win.
Flags of unity
are now advancing.
And you will come
marching together with me,
and so you'll see
your song and your flag blossom.
The light
of a red dawn
already announces
the life to come.

Arise, fight
the people are going to win.
The life to come
will be better.
To conquer
our happiness.
and a clamor
of a thousand fighting voices will rise, speaking
a song of freedom.
With determination
the people will win.

And now the people,
who are rising in struggle
with a giant voice
crying out: Forward!
