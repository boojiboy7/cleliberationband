About this song

"Ana Dammi Falastini" (translation: "My Blood is Palestinian") is a 2015 song by Mohammed Assaf, a Palestinian pop singer and winner of the second season of Arab Idol. The song is based on several traditional Palestinian styles and has become a global anthem for the Palestinian people and the continued resistance against Zionist occupation.
