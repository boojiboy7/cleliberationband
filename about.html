About

We are a group of Cleveland area musicians of any and all skill levels, dedicated to playing songs for collective liberation.

Can I join the band?

Yes! We are always looking for new members! Our lineup changes with almost every show, and that's perfect. If you want to join us, you can start practicing a song right now. When you are ready, reach out to us on our Instagram for more info on practice times and schedules.
